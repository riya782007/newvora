Questions businesses ask us

What is custom software?

Software designed and built for one business's specific operations, rather than a ready-made product that every company uses the same way. You decide the screens, the steps and the reports.

How much does custom software cost?

It depends on how many workflows the software covers. We first understand your operations, then send a written scope and a fixed quote. You approve it before any work begins, so there are no surprises mid-project.

Who owns the software and the code?

You do. The code, the data and the accounts belong to your business.

Can you connect it to Tally, WhatsApp or our existing tools?

Yes. Integrations with the tools you already use are a normal part of our projects, and we confirm each one during scoping.

Do you work only in Delhi?

We are based in Delhi and meet Delhi NCR clients in person. Clients elsewhere in India work with us over calls and WhatsApp.

Tell us what you need

Message us however you like. We reply personally and start by understanding your business, not by selling.
