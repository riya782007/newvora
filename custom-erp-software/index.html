Questions businesses ask us

Custom ERP or ready-made ERP: which is better for a small business?

Ready-made ERP is quicker to start but makes your team adapt to its process, and you pay for modules you never use. Custom ERP costs more upfront but fits your exact workflow and grows module by module. If your process is standard, a ready-made product may be enough, and we will tell you so.

How much does custom ERP software cost in India?

The cost depends on the number of modules and how complex your workflows are. We scope your business first, then give you a fixed written quote. Many clients start with two or three core modules and add more later.

Can it work with Tally and GST billing?

Yes. GST-ready invoices and exporting entries to Tally are common requirements, and we confirm the exact setup during scoping.

Can my team use it on mobile?

Yes. We build it as a web app that works on phones, tablets and computers, so staff can use it in the godown, the shop or the field.

What happens after the ERP is delivered?

We hand over everything, train your team, and stay available for support and improvements.
