Questions businesses ask us

How long does a business website take?

The timeline depends on the number of pages and features. It is written into your quote before we start.

Will my website show up on Google?

We build every site with the technical structure Google needs: fast pages, clear titles, a sitemap and schema markup. Ranking also depends on reviews and other sites mentioning you, and we guide you on those too.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes, if you want to. We can set it up so you can edit text and photos, or we can handle updates for you.

Do you also do domain and email setup?

Yes. We connect your domain, set up professional email addresses and make sure they land in inboxes, not spam.
