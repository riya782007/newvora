Questions businesses ask us

Should I use Shopify or a custom platform?

If you sell standard retail products, Shopify is often the fastest start, and we can build on it. If you need wholesale tiers, credit buyers or a custom ordering flow, a custom platform usually fits better. We recommend one after understanding your business.

Can wholesale buyers see different prices from retail customers?

Yes. Separate price lists, minimum quantities and buyer-specific pricing are common in the platforms we build.

Which payment methods can you add?

UPI, cards, net banking through a payment gateway, and cash on delivery.

Have you built this before?

Yes. Our work includes a jewellery wholesale commerce platform with retail and wholesale tiers, variant inventory and UPI/COD checkout. Ask us on WhatsApp for a walkthrough.
